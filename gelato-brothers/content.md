## The Brogato® — Our Pour-Over Affogato

### Section headline
The Brogato®: An Italian Classic, Poured Our Way

### Copy
In Italy, they call it the *affogato* — "drowned." A scoop of gelato,
drowned in a hot shot of espresso, eaten slowly at a marble counter while
the whole afternoon waits for you. It's dessert and coffee and a small
ceremony all at once, and Italians have been perfecting it for generations.

We fell in love with it in the beachfront cafés of Europe, and we brought
it home with one promise: never rush the pour.

Our version is the **Brogato®** — two scoops of Stracciatella gelato
(Madagascar vanilla bean laced with shards of mild Italian chocolate),
finished tableside with a fresh-pulled crema espresso poured over the top.
The hot espresso melts into the cold gelato in ribbons, the crema does the
rest, and for about ninety seconds you understand exactly why Italians
never invented the to-go affogato.

Breakfast, lunch, dinner, or the excuse in between — the Brogato® is the
European sweet treat that works at any hour.

**Pull quote:** "Affogato" means "drowned." We just call it the best
ninety seconds of your day.

### Build (menu detail)
1. Two scoops of Stracciatella gelato, made in-house
2. A double shot of espresso, pulled fresh on our Italian machine
3. Poured over the top the moment it hits your hands — never before
4. Optional: a Brodallion® waffle chip on the side for scooping

## Brodallions® — Waffle Chips from Scratch, Eggs from the Farm

### Section headline
Baked from Scratch. Laid This Morning.

### Copy
Every Brodallion® — our signature waffle chip — starts the same way: with
a walk out to the coop.

We keep chickens and ducks right here on our family farm, and their eggs
go straight into our scratch-made waffle batter. No cartons, no
middleman, no mix. Farm eggs, real butter, Madagascar vanilla, and a hot
waffle iron — that's the whole ingredient story. Duck eggs run richer than
chicken eggs, with bigger yolks and more fat, and that's what gives our
chips their deep golden color and a crisp-then-tender bite you can't fake
with anything from a box.

We bake them fresh in our Tennessee bakery, the same way the waffle stands
in Europe do it: small batches, all day, so the smell of hot vanilla
batter is part of the experience before you ever reach the counter.

Crumble them over gelato. Dunk them in espresso. Use one as the only
spoon a Brogato® ever needed. Or just eat the whole sleeve in the car —
we won't tell.

**Pull quote:** From our coop to your cone — the eggs in every Brodallion®
were laid by our own chickens and ducks at the farm.

- 🧇 Scratch-baked daily — never from a mix
- 🌿 Madagascar vanilla, real butter, simple ingredients

## Our Story (supporting section)

It started with family summers on the European coast — espresso in the
air, waffle irons going all day, gelato cases glowing like stained glass.
We watched whole villages slow down for dessert, and we couldn't stop
asking: why doesn't home feel like this?

So we brought it back to East Tennessee — the recipes, the Italian
espresso machine, and the stubborn insistence on doing things the slow
way. Today that means gelato churned in small batches, espresso pulled
to order, and waffle chips baked from scratch with eggs gathered from our
own chickens and ducks at the farm.

Two traditions, one counter: Italian craft, Tennessee farm.
