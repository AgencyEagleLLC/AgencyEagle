Il Brogato® · Our Pour-Over Affogato

The Brogato®: an Italian classic, poured our way

Affogato — “drowned” — the way Italy has served dessert for generations.

In Italy, they call it the affogato — “drowned.” A scoop of gelato, drowned in a hot shot of espresso, eaten slowly at a marble counter while the whole afternoon waits for you. It’s dessert and coffee and a small ceremony all at once, and Italians have been perfecting it for generations.

We fell in love with it in the beachfront cafés of Europe, and we brought it home with one promise: never rush the pour.

Our version is the Brogato® — two scoops of Stracciatella gelato (Madagascar vanilla bean laced with shards of mild Italian chocolate), finished with a fresh-pulled crema espresso poured over the top. The hot espresso melts into the cold gelato in ribbons, the crema does the rest, and for about ninety seconds you understand exactly why Italians never invented the to-go affogato.

Breakfast, lunch, dinner, or the excuse in between — the Brogato® is the European sweet treat that works at any hour.

  1. Two scoops of Stracciatella gelato, made in-house
  2. A double shot of espresso, pulled fresh on our Italian machine
  3. Poured over the top the moment it hits your hands — never before
  4. Optional: a Brodallion® waffle chip on the side for scooping
“Affogato” means “drowned.” We just call it the best ninety seconds of your day.

I Brodallions® · Our Signature Waffle Chips

Baked from scratch. Laid this morning.

Fatto a mano — handmade, from our coop to your cone.

Every Brodallion® — our signature waffle chip — starts the same way: with a walk out to the coop.

We keep chickens and ducks right here on our family farm, and their eggs go straight into our scratch-made waffle batter. No cartons, no middleman, no mix. Farm eggs, real butter, Madagascar vanilla, and a hot waffle iron — that’s the whole ingredient story. Duck eggs run richer than chicken eggs, with bigger yolks and more fat, and that’s what gives our chips their deep golden color and a crisp-then-tender bite you can’t fake with anything from a box.

We bake them fresh in our Tennessee bakery, the same way the waffle stands in Europe do it: small batches, all day, so the smell of hot vanilla batter is part of the experience before you ever reach the counter.

Crumble them over gelato. Dunk them in espresso. Use one as the only spoon a Brogato® ever needed. Or just eat the whole sleeve in the car — we won’t tell.

La Nostra Storia · Our Story

Two traditions, one counter

It started with family summers on the European coast — espresso in the air, waffle irons going all day, gelato cases glowing like stained glass. We watched whole villages slow down for dessert, and we couldn’t stop asking: why doesn’t home feel like this?

So we brought it back to East Tennessee — the recipes, the Italian espresso machine, and the stubborn insistence on doing things the slow way. Today that means gelato churned in small batches, espresso pulled to order, and waffle chips baked from scratch with eggs gathered from our own chickens and ducks at the farm.

Italian craft. Tennessee farm.
