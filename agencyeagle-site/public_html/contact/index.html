Our story

Agency Eagle has helped hundreds of businesses across the United States and Canada with sales, service and marketing since 2002. We started as Laguna Internet Technologies in the rolling hills of East Tennessee and became Agency Eagle in January 2014.

Today we focus on custom website design and hosting. We intend to stay small. Behind the curtain is a trusted team of talent we call on regularly, which keeps our sites fresh and lets us grow with each project. No job is too big, and we're always glad to hear from great people who'd like to work with us.
