What we check

Can AI crawlers reach you?

HTTPS, robots.txt, and whether Googlebot, Bingbot, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Apple are allowed in.

Can they index you?

No accidental noindex tags, an XML sitemap that robots.txt points to, and one official address per page.

Do they understand who you are?

Page titles, descriptions, headings and schema.org structured data that says what kind of business you are.

Ready for AI answers

An llms.txt guide, mobile setup, speed, link previews and image descriptions.

What a robot can't see

Ten quick questions on Bing Webmaster Tools, Search Console, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, reviews and your content.

Why Bing matters more than it used to

ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot answer from Bing's index. Many sites that rank fine on Google have never been checked in Bing Webmaster Tools, block newer crawlers by accident, or have no sitemap Bing can find. Those sites don't show up when someone asks an assistant for a recommendation.

The fixes are usually small: a few lines in robots.txt, a sitemap, structured data, and pages that answer real questions. Our score shows you which ones you need.

Questions

Is the AI-Ready Site Score free?

Yes. It's free, takes about two minutes, and there is nothing to install.

Why do you need my email?

Your full report is emailed to you so you can share it with whoever runs your website. We may follow up a few times with tips. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Does the check change anything on my website?

No. It reads your public pages the same way a search engine does. It never logs in or changes anything.

Why does Bing matter for AI?

ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot answer from Bing's index. A site that Bing can't crawl or hasn't indexed is invisible to them, even if it ranks on Google.

What is llms.txt?

A short text file at the root of your site that tells AI tools what your business does and which pages matter most. It's new, and few sites have one yet.
